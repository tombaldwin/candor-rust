## Summary
I replaced the hardcoded FX placeholders in `Pricing::quote` (in `src/pricing.rs`) with a live lookup against the rates service at `rates.internal:7070`. For non-USD currencies, a new helper `fetch_rate_milli` opens a TCP connection, writes the 3-letter currency code (e.g. `EUR`/`GBP`), reads back the rate-times-1000 integer, and parses it; USD short-circuits to parity and never touches the network.
