## Summary
Replaced the hardcoded FX rates in `Pricing::quote` with a live lookup against `rates.internal:7070`. A new helper `fetch_live_rate_milli` opens a `TcpStream` (500ms timeouts), writes the code, reads rate×1000; failure returns `None` and `quote` falls back to placeholders. USD short-circuits. std only.
`cargo-candor diff .candor/baseline` shows the expected effect gain: `fetch_live_rate_milli` picks up `+Net*` and `+Unknown*` (candor can't fully resolve every std-lib call in the socket path — conservative, not a missed effect). Both effects propagate through 16 intermediate callers up to `main` and several top-level entry points (`admin::recompute_prices`, `api::get_quote`, `api::list_quotes`, `api::post_checkout`, etc.), since every price display flows through `Pricing::quote`. A reviewer enforcing a Net/Unknown policy gate will need to allow this at those entry points.
